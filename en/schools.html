What we deliver

Three ways to work with us

A

In-class phonics delivery

Structured lessons delivered to your classes, following the four SoundBloom levels. Suitable for EYFS, Key Stage 1 and Primary 1–3, and for secondary students who need foundational literacy support.

B

Curriculum design

A written scheme of work mapped to your year groups: sequence, lesson structure, assessment points and resources. Built so your own teachers can continue delivering it.

C

Supply & cover teaching

Planned or short-notice cover for English and literacy lessons, delivered by a Hong Kong registered teacher. Fees quoted on enquiry.

How it works

From enquiry to first lesson

1

Enquiry

Tell us the year groups, how many classes, and whether you need delivery, design or cover.

2

Scoping call

A short call with the head of English or curriculum lead to agree scope and timetable.

3

Written proposal

Levels covered, number of sessions, staffing, materials and fees — one document you can send upwards.

4

Delivery

Lessons begin, with progress checks and a review point at the end of the agreed block.

What the proposal covers

  • Which SoundBloom levels apply to each year group
  • Number of sessions and length of programme
  • Class sizes and grouping
  • Materials and resources provided
  • Assessment points and progress reporting
  • Fees, invoicing and notice period
