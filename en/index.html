Our approach

Sound first. Then words.
Then reading that sticks.

Every lesson is built so a child hears it, says it, reads it and spells it — in that order. Nothing is skipped, nothing is rushed.

1

Hear the sound

Accurate pronunciation of single sounds and digraphs, modelled and repeated until it is automatic.

2

Build the word

Blending to read, segmenting to spell. CVC words first, then blends, then vowel teams.

3

Read for meaning

Guided reading, fluency and comprehension — so decoding turns into understanding.

The programme

Four levels, one clear path

Each level has a fixed set of skills and a stated outcome. You will know exactly what your child can do before moving up. The four levels are the phonics and reading pathway; Secondary English is a separate one-to-one service.

Level 1

Sound Foundations

Beginner readers
  • Single-letter sounds
  • Blending to read
  • CVC words & letter formation
  • High-frequency words
Learning goalReads and spells simple words independently.
Level 2

Sound Connections

Building fluency
  • Consonant digraphs
  • Initial & final blends
  • Four- and five-sound words
  • Longer sentences & punctuation
Learning goalDecodes longer words and reads sentences accurately.
Level 3

Vowel Pathways

Growing reader
  • Long-vowel patterns
  • Vowel teams & split vowels
  • Two-syllable words
  • Fluency and expression
Learning goalReads short passages fluently and with feeling.
Level 4

Reading & Spelling Mastery

Independent learner
  • Complex sound–letter patterns
  • Prefixes, suffixes, morphology
  • Multisyllabic strategies
  • Comprehension & paragraph writing
Learning goalApplies strategies independently across texts.
