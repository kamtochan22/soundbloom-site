How billing works

Fees are invoiced in four-lesson blocks. The block price is exactly four times the per-lesson fee — it is a billing unit, not a discount.

Start with a free 15-minute parent consultation. We will recommend a level and confirm what it costs before you commit to anything.

Questions

Before you enquire

How big are the groups?
Three to five students. It is capped deliberately: in a smaller group every child reads aloud in every lesson, which is the only way pronunciation and fluency actually improve.
Is the 4-lesson block cheaper?
No. The block is four lessons at the per-lesson rate, billed together. We would rather show you the real number than a package price that hides it.
Online or in person?
Both. One-to-one can be online or at our Kwai Fong venue. Group classes run at the centre.
Do you teach secondary students?
Yes. Secondary English is one-to-one, $500 per lesson, and focuses on reading, writing and the literacy gaps that hold students back in other subjects.
How do we start?
A free 15-minute parent consultation. Tell us your child's age and what they find hard; we will say where to start and what it costs. No obligation.
Do you work with schools?
Yes — school-based delivery, curriculum design and supply & cover teaching from HK$800 per hour. See For Schools.
